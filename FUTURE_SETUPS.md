## US dollar breakdown (DXY, eToro: USDOLLAR, id 25)

**Thesis.** A weaker dollar helps the US (exports, debt costs) and China and other
dollar borrowers, so policy is likely to push it lower over time. When the dollar breaks
down, expect a slow decline lasting months or longer, not a quick drop.

**Chart (as of 2 Oct 2026).** Fell from about 110 to 96 in the first half of 2025, then
went sideways in a range of about 95.2 to 101.9 for 15 months. Range floor: 95.2–96.4
(lows Jul 2025, Sep 2025, Jan 2026, Feb 2026). Range ceiling: 101.3–101.9 (highs Jun 2026,
Jul 2026, late Sep 2026). On 2 Oct 2026 it was 101.6, at the top of the range.

**Trigger.** A weekly close below about 95, ideally confirmed by gold, EUR/USD and
Bitcoin moving the same way. A rise above 102 means the idea is on hold.

**Entry.** Wait for the retest: price comes back up to 95–96 from below and is turned
down. Skip it if it never retests.

**Stop.** From the weekly chart: above the retest zone and the latest weekly swing high.
Size the position from that stop.

**Style.** A position trade held for months, judged on weekly closes. Low or no leverage.
Leveraged positions and CFDs (short USDOLLAR, leveraged EUR/USD) pay overnight fees every
night, which add up over months, so prefer ways to own the idea without fees:
unleveraged gold or silver, crypto without leverage, or a dollar-bearish ETF if eToro
lists one.

**Shorter-term variant.** A rejection at the range ceiling (a daily or weekly close
turning down from 101.3–101.9) is a separate short: stop above about 102.4, target the
range floor around 96.5. It follows the normal trading rules.

**Conflicts.** A short on silver is effectively a bet on a stronger dollar, so it works
against this idea. Crypto holdings already gain when the dollar weakens.
